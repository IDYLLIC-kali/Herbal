Our Story

We started Betti Naturals because the oils and herbs we wanted for ourselves weren't on any shelf without being mixed with something else, or sold under a label that was vague about where it came from.

So we began with three oils and a few dried herbs, sold in plain jars to friends and neighbours. Everything was one ingredient. Everything was bought in small quantities. Everything was packed when someone asked for it.

That's still how we work. Four categories; oils, herbs & powders, teas, seeds & nuts and one rule for each product: if we can't tell you exactly what's in the jar, we don't stock it.
